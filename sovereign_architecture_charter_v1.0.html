1. Executive Declaration

The Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus is conceived as an integrated regenerative development platform designed to demonstrate:

Food Sovereignty

Planned livestock production supported by biosecurity, animal welfare, nursery and operational controls.

Energy Sovereignty

Solar PV, battery storage, biogas and combined heat and power (CHP), coordinated through a campus microgrid.

Water Sovereignty

On-site water supply and storage, subject to sustainable yield, water quality and authorisation checks.

Accessibility Sovereignty

Inclusive routes, facilities, charging provisions and emergency arrangements designed for diverse mobility needs.

Skills Sovereignty

Operator learning, practical training, entrepreneurship and skills transfer.

Circular Resource Recovery

Resource efficiency, nutrient recovery and future integration of additional circular-economy systems.

2. Design Authority & Governance

This Charter is the controlling internal baseline for campus planning. Supporting studies, financial models, engineering packages, bills of quantities (BOQs), procurement plans and expansion proposals shall identify the Charter version from which they derive.

Change-control rule

No material change to capacity, system configuration, safety approach, cost basis or operating assumptions should be treated as approved until its rationale, impacts, evidence and approval status have been recorded.

7.1 Electricity valuation assumption

Planning value: R1.80/kWh. Retain this as the fixed baseline assumption for comparable future offset calculations until formally revised. It is a modelling input, not a guarantee of the actual tariff or avoided cost.

7.2 Microgrid logic

Solar PV+BESS+Biogas CHP=Sovereign Microgrid

Priority loads: nursery, water supply, SCADA, biosecurity and essential livestock services.

Baseline: 30 kWp PV, 60 kWh LFP BESS, 70 m³ biodigester and 15 kW CHP. Expandable: staged PV, storage, generation and load-management upgrades. Measured: generation, consumption, imports/exports where applicable, battery state of charge, runtime, fuel/biogas use, uptime and verified avoided cost. Review trigger: critical-load shortfall, repeated outages, sustained utilisation above threshold or a material change in load profile.

Complete an hourly/seasonal energy balance, critical-load schedule, inverter and protection design, islanding/black-start strategy, power-quality assessment, gas-yield assessment and electrical/fire safety review. “Energy sovereignty” is the design objective; actual grid independence must be demonstrated by engineering analysis and commissioning.

Nursery functions

  • Recovery
  • Isolation
  • Veterinary support
  • Thermal recovery

Biosecurity controls

  • Single controlled entry point
  • Vehicle screening
  • Personnel screening
  • Shower/change transition as specified by the biosecurity plan
  • Eight stated biosecure pods across poultry and swine clusters
  • Isolation capability

Design objective: zero-mortality-oriented design, understood as a continuous improvement aspiration rather than a guarantee. Track mortality and morbidity against veterinary targets and investigate every material deviation.

Measured: mortality, morbidity, environmental conditions, water/feed intake, cleaning compliance, access records and isolation events. Review trigger: disease alert, unusual mortality, biosecurity breach, inadequate environmental conditions or insufficient isolation capacity.

20. Final Declaration

This Charter establishes the internal Design Authority for the Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus.

All future feasibility studies, financial models, funding applications, engineering calculations, bills of quantities, procurement plans, operating procedures and expansion projects shall identify and derive from the applicable approved baseline.

Nothing is discarded. Everything is versioned. Everything is measurable. Everything is expandable. Everything is teachable. Everything is regenerative.

Document principle: preserve the vision, validate the assumptions, control the changes and measure the results.
